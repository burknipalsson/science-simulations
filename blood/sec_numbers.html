Honest numbers

Checked against experiments

Before any graphics, each part of the model was run headless and compared with measurements or exact theory: first the membrane alone, then the fluid alone, then the two together, then crowds of cells.

Most of it agrees within a few per cent. The places where it doesn't are listed too: a red cell pulled by laser tweezers narrows less than real ones do at low force, the cells crowd toward the middle of a tube less than in glass-tube experiments, and the live arteriole's coarse grid reads 15–20% thick.

Sources: Evans & Fung 1972; Mills et al. 2004 (via Suresh 2005 data); Barthès-Biesel & Rallison 1981 and Krüger et al. 2011; Pries et al. 1992; Fedosov et al. 2010 (Microcirculation 17:615).
