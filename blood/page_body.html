Inside a Drop of Blood

A drop of blood holds about five million red cells per microlitre, each a soft bag of haemoglobin wider than the narrowest vessels it must pass. Here the plasma is a real fluid simulation and every cell is an elastic membrane with the measured stiffness of a red cell, solved together on your graphics card.
