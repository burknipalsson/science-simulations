What you are looking at

The box above is about 22 nanometres across, a hundred times smaller than a virus. The stripes are the electron's wave, its crests and troughs about 0.17 nm apart, the size of an atom. Nothing is drawn by hand: each frame is the Schrödinger equation stepped forward by a few hundredths of a femtosecond, on a grid of a million points.
