The simplest chemical bond · H₂⁺

One electron, two protons

Bring a second proton near a hydrogen atom and the electron's wave spreads over both. If it settles in the bonding shape, it piles up between the protons and its pull on each of them outweighs their repulsion: the two are held together. This is the hydrogen molecular ion, the one molecule whose Schrödinger equation can be solved exactly, and every chemical bond is a more crowded version of it.
