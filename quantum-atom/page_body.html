The Quantum Atom

Below a nanometre, matter stops behaving like little balls. An electron travels as a wave, spreading through both slits of a screen, leaking through walls, and settling around a nucleus in shapes no planet could follow. This is the Schrödinger equation solved live on your graphics card, a million points at a time.
