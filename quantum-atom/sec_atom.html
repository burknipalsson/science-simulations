Where the electron lives

Put the electron wave in a box of its own making, the pull of a single proton, and it can only settle into certain shapes, each with an exact energy. These are the orbitals chemistry is built from. They are not paths; they are standing waves, and the brightness shows where the electron is likely to be found. Everything here comes straight from the solution of Schrödinger's equation for hydrogen, drawn in three dimensions on your graphics card.
